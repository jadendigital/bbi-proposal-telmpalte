The home

A generous two-storey family home in one of the Shire's most tightly held pockets. It presents well and lives large, with flexible living, a wide back wall of glass and beautiful northern light through the day. Our job is to draw the eye to the space and that light, and to soften the rooms so they feel like a home rather than a floorplan.

The likely buyer

Established Sutherland Shire families and upgraders, drawn to the lifestyle, the schools and the closeness to the water at Port Hacking and the beaches. They are buying a family future, so we style for warmth, ease and a sense of room to grow.

Preparing the home

Before install day we plan the home together, what stays, what moves to another room, and what is best set aside for the campaign. Most of it is your larger lounge and bedroom pieces and the personal touches that make the home yours rather than the buyer's.

One plan, one page

The room notes below carry the detail, so you and our install team are reading from the same page. Nothing is moved or set aside without it being agreed with you first.

We style to attract the widest qualified pool of buyers, never to a single taste, so the home speaks to as many of the right people as possible while still feeling specific to this property.
